Route/course validation
Checks whether the ship's current coordinates lie on the route between an origin and destination port. Use this to reject invalid positions that fall far away from the intended course, such as inland coordinates or sea coordinates in the wrong region.
Example: if a vessel is travelling from Visakhapatnam to Singapore, then coordinates near that route should be valid, while coordinates like Delhi or Hyderabad should be rejected as off-route.
